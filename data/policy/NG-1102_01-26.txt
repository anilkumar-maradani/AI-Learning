Title: Windstorm, Hurricane and Hail Endorsement

Issued by Northgate Mutual Insurance Company. This endorsement changes the
Northgate Homeowners Policy for loss caused by windstorm, hurricane or hail.

SECTION I — WHAT THIS ENDORSEMENT COVERS

CLAUSE WH-1 — HURRICANE DEDUCTIBLE
When the loss is caused by a hurricane that the National Hurricane Center has
given a name, the hurricane deductible replaces the all-peril deductible. The
hurricane deductible is two percent (2%) of the Coverage A limit shown in the
Declarations and applies once per hurricane. For any other windstorm or hail
loss the all-peril deductible applies.

CLAUSE WH-2 — RAIN THAT ENTERS THE HOME
We cover damage to the inside of the dwelling from rain, snow or sleet only if
wind or hail first made an opening in the roof or an outside wall and the
water came in through that opening.

CLAUSE WH-3 — OLDER ROOF SURFACES
If the roof surface was more than fifteen (15) years old on the date of loss,
we pay the actual cash value of the roof surface, not its replacement cost.
The adjuster records the actual cash value on the claim file. Damage to the
rest of the dwelling is still paid at replacement cost.

SECTION III — HOW WE PAY
We subtract the deductible that applies under WH-1 from the covered loss.
