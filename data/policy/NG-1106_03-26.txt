Policy Line: homeowners
Effective Date: March 1, 2026
Title: Home Business Property Endorsement

Issued by Northgate Mutual Insurance Company. This endorsement covers a
limited amount of property used for a business run from the home.

SECTION I — WHAT THIS ENDORSEMENT COVERS

CLAUSE HB-1 — BUSINESS PROPERTY AT HOME
We cover property used in your business while it is at the residence premises,
for the same causes of loss the policy covers for your personal property. The
most we pay is a sublimit of $2,500 per occurrence. We subtract the all-peril
deductible first, then cap the result at $2,500.

CLAUSE HB-2 — BUSINESS PROPERTY AWAY FROM HOME
Business property is covered away from the residence premises up to a
sublimit of $500 per occurrence, after the deductible.

CLAUSE HB-3 — WHAT COUNTS AS A BUSINESS
A business is any trade, profession or occupation you carry on for money,
including work you do at home as an employee of someone else.
