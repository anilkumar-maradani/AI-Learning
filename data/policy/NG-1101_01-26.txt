Title: Escape of Water Endorsement

Issued by Northgate Mutual Insurance Company. This endorsement changes the
Northgate Homeowners Policy. Read it together with the Declarations, which
show the all-peril deductible and the Coverage A limit.

SECTION I — WHAT THIS ENDORSEMENT COVERS

CLAUSE WE-1 — SUDDEN ESCAPE OF WATER
We cover direct physical damage to the dwelling and to personal property
caused by the sudden and accidental escape of water or steam from a plumbing,
heating, air conditioning or fire sprinkler system, or from a household
appliance. An escape is "sudden" only if it continued for fewer than ten (10)
days before it was discovered. An escape that continued for ten days or more
is treated as seepage under exclusion E-41.

CLAUSE WE-2 — ACCESS AND TEAR-OUT
When a covered escape of water happens inside a wall, floor or ceiling, we
also pay the reasonable cost to open and restore that part of the building so
the system can be reached. Access and tear-out is limited to a sublimit of
$5,000 per occurrence.

CLAUSE WE-3 — HOW WE PAY
The all-peril deductible shown in the Declarations applies once per
occurrence. We subtract the deductible from the covered loss first. Where a
sublimit applies, we then cap the result at that sublimit.

SECTION III — YOUR DUTIES AFTER A LOSS
You must shut off the water supply as soon as you discover the escape and
report the loss to us promptly. Keep the failed part for our inspection.
