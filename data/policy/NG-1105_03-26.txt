Title: Earth Movement Exclusion Endorsement

Issued by Northgate Mutual Insurance Company. This endorsement defines earth
movement and explains how it interacts with other covered causes of loss.

SECTION I — DEFINITIONS AND RULES

CLAUSE GM-1 — WHAT EARTH MOVEMENT MEANS
Earth movement means earthquake, landslide, mudflow, sinkhole collapse,
subsidence, and the sinking, rising or shifting of the ground, whether it
happens naturally or is caused by people.

CLAUSE GM-2 — LOSS THAT FOLLOWS EARTH MOVEMENT
If earth movement causes a fire or an explosion, we cover the damage done by
that fire or explosion.

CLAUSE GM-3 — MORE THAN ONE CAUSE
A loss caused by earth movement is excluded even when another cause, such as
an escape of water or a windstorm, contributed at the same time or in any
order. This includes water that escapes from a pipe that broke because the
ground moved.
