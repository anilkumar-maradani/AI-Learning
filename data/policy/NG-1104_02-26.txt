Title: Valuable Articles Endorsement

Issued by Northgate Mutual Insurance Company. This endorsement covers jewelry,
watches, art and similar articles, whether or not they are listed on the
Valuable Articles Schedule attached to the Declarations.

SECTION I — WHAT THIS ENDORSEMENT COVERS

CLAUSE VA-1 — SCHEDULED ARTICLES
An article listed on the Valuable Articles Schedule is insured for the agreed
value shown on the schedule. No deductible applies to a scheduled article.

CLAUSE VA-2 — LOSS ANYWHERE, INCLUDING DISAPPEARANCE
Scheduled articles are covered anywhere in the world. We cover a scheduled
article that is lost or that disappears in a way you cannot explain, even
when no theft can be shown.

CLAUSE VA-3 — ARTICLES NOT ON THE SCHEDULE
Jewelry and watches that are not on the schedule are covered for theft only,
up to a sublimit of $1,500 per occurrence. We subtract the all-peril
deductible first, then cap the result at $1,500.
