Title: Mold and Wet Rot Limited Coverage Endorsement

Issued by Northgate Mutual Insurance Company. The base Northgate Homeowners
Policy excludes mold. This endorsement gives back a limited amount of cover.

SECTION I — WHAT THIS ENDORSEMENT COVERS

CLAUSE MR-1 — WHEN MOLD IS COVERED
We cover mold, fungi and wet rot only when both of these are true:
(a) the mold came from a water loss that is itself covered by the policy,
such as a sudden escape of water under NG-1101 WE-1; and
(b) you began drying the affected area within seventy-two (72) hours of
discovering the water.

CLAUSE MR-2 — MOLD SUBLIMIT
The most we pay for all mold, fungi and wet rot in one policy period is
$7,500. This sublimit includes testing, removal, tear-out and rebuilding.
We subtract the all-peril deductible from the covered mold loss first, then
cap the result at $7,500.
